Commissions Guidelines

Will Do

Gorilla Tag Style I specialize in creating custom profile photos, banners, and character scenes styled exactly like GTAG monkeys.
Complex Cosmetics Includes complete head, body, hand, and facial cosmetics. Detail options like custom badges, holdables, and customized patterns.
Kinitopet & Digital Critters I can draw flat and retro-styled digital creatures inspired by classic 2000s desktop companion aesthetics.
Etc. (Custom Requests) Open to alternative mascot designs or simplistic flat-vector layouts within my current illustration scope.

Will Not Do

NSFW / 18+ Content I run a clean, friendly content channel and do not design explicit, suggestive, or highly mature themes under any circumstances.
Realistic Humans My visual style is highly optimized for stylized, low-poly cartoon avatars, flat designs, and animals. Realism or anatomy studies are outside my scope.
Heavy Gore Light cuts or basic stylized bruises are fine, but I do not draw intense, realistic violence, gore, or horror elements.
Excluded Categories If a theme or design structure is not clearly specified in my "Will Do" category, I reserve the right to decline or suggest modifications.
