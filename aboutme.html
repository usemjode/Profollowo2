About Me

My Creative Journey

Youtuber Since Jan 4, 2021

From Roblox to VR Mastery

I first launched my YouTube channel on January 4, 2021, as an experimental creative space. My early uploads were driven by absolute curiosity, sharing fun and lighthearted gameplay moments on platforms like Roblox while capturing early gaming memories.

Over the years, my passion evolved as I dived deep into the Gorilla Tag and Kats communities. Focusing on these high-energy virtual spaces pushed me to transform standard recording clips into sleek, heavily edited projects.

My Video Editing Evolution

01

CapCut (The Beginning)

Started my path working with mobile-friendly timelines. This tool taught me the essential rhythm of sync-cuts, basic transitions, and pacing sound effects to high-tempo gameplay tracks.

02

DaVinci Resolve (Going Professional)

Moved up to a desktop workflow to master complex nodes, tracking, and advanced color correction. This phase vastly polished the raw visual depth and clarity of my VR edits.

03

Premiere Pro (The Current Standard)

My primary engine for production today. It allows me to construct ultra-slick keyframed dynamics, seamless audio mixing, and rapid render pipelines for high-retention social content.
